A little about me

Oh Hey There,
I'm Kelly

I'm a coach, a mum of teenagers, a keeper of dogs and chickens, and someone who spent a long time being much better at showing up for everyone else than for myself.

My background is in healthcare. Nearly 15 years as a Registered Anaesthetic Technician across clinical, leadership and education roles has taught me a lot about people, pressure, and what it actually takes to keep functioning under the weight of many hats. It also eventually taught me that functioning and flourishing aren't the same thing either.

Unfold grew out of my own process of slowing down, laying it all out, and getting honest about what actually mattered. That process is what I bring to every session.

How we work together

Sometimes you just need somewhere
to put it all down for a bit.

Coaching with me is an opportunity to step back from the pace of things, let your mind settle, and have a proper look at what's going on. Together we figure out what you're carrying that might be put down, or at least carried differently.

We work at your pace. You decide when it's enough.
