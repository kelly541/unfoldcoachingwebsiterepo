About UnFold

We are all human...

At Unfold I offer nervous-system centred coaching and a calm, shame-free space to get honest about life and how you are navigating it.

The coaching support I offer is informed by lived experience and backed by my ANZCAL coaching accreditation, RYT 200, upcoming Integrative Somatic certification and an almost 15-year career in healthcare. I have walked this path professionally and personally, and I know it looks different for everyone.

I work with a wide variety of people from parents and professionals through to leaders and humans holding it all together under the weight of many hats.

Together we:

  • • Uncover where the overwhelm is actually showing up and why
  • • Explore the gap between who you genuinely are and who you feel you have to be
  • • Get clear on your values, your strengths and the beliefs that have been quietly holding you back
  • • Build boundaries that are meaningful, felt and yours

By gently bringing the mind and body into the work together, we can support your nervous-system while gaining the clarity to lead life as the most authentic and sustainable version of you.

"Capability and capacity are not the same thing, and knowing the difference changes everything. It isn't about caring less, it is about making space to keep giving your energy, just to the right things."
