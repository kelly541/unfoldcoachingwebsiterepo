A little about me

Oh Hey There,
I'm Kelly...

I am a wife, a mum of teenagers, keeper of dogs and chickens, a lifelong learner and an all-round imperfect human. When I'm not coaching you'll find me with family and friends, walking and hiking in nature, failing with joy in improv and acting classes, and generally embracing a bit of tomfoolery wherever possible. I believe that life doesn't just happen to us, we also happen to life, and while we often forget this, it is a belief that can be incredibly empowering.

Professionally, my background is in healthcare. An almost 15-year career as a Registered Anaesthetic Technician across clinical, leadership and education roles taught me a great deal about people, pressure and the privilege of walking alongside others at their most vulnerable. It also taught me something equally important: how we show up for and as ourselves matters just as much.

Like most people who give a lot, I was better at the former than the latter for a long time. Alongside a demanding career I navigated grief, sudden loss, debilitating health challenges and the particular kind of pressure that comes from navigating personal and family life while neglecting to keep enough in reserve for yourself. I did what capable people so often do, I held it together and kept going. I'm clearly both incredibly stubborn and a ridiculously slow learner, but eventually the right question found me: was I still heading in the right direction?

That question found me in 2024. What followed was a process of stripping back inherited patterns and perceived expectations, and coming home to what was actually mine. Because we cannot think our way to wholeness. Real change happens when we come back to the body, back to what we feel underneath all the noise, back to what actually matters. Getting honest about my values, my direction and my way of moving through the world gave me something I hadn't had in years: a solid and authentic foundation to move forward from.

That process is what Unfold is built on.

I am an ANZCAL Registered Coach, 200hr RYT and currently completing my 300hr Integrative Somatic Facilitation training. I bring a warm, supportive and confidential space to every session, where a sense of humour is not only welcome but often necessary. I will always meet you where you are, working at your pace and in a way that feels right for you.
